Four screens with demo teammates: People, approving a new machine, signing in from a terminal, and the one-time claim that moves today's setup over. The colours are the P16.30 dark layers.

Idea: you're one person with one passkey, and "owner" means owner of the fleet. Devices are listed under you rather than as separate users, and machines never appear here. Teammates get a fleet-wide role, which you can narrow per machine.

2 · A new machine asks you to be its owner

Idea: run herder install on the server, then scan its QR code (or open the link). The code on the phone has to match the one in the terminal, so nobody else can claim the machine first. Face ID signs it, and from then on all your devices can use the machine.

3 · Signing in from a terminal (no passkey there)

Idea: the terminal shows a QR code, your phone approves it with Face ID, and the terminal gets its own device credential tied to you. The passkey never leaves your phone.

tomas@trash-can-01 ~ $ herder login Sign in to herder as a person. Scan with your phone, or open herder.sh/l/K7Q4PD Code on your phone should read K7Q · 4PD … waiting for your phone ✓ Signed in as Tomas (owner of 5 machines). This terminal is listed under your devices.

4 · The one-time move: "Claim these machines as me"

Idea: run once from your Mac, which is already an owner everywhere. Each old per-machine "tomas" becomes your passkey. The machine-as-user rows (trash-can-01, Coingate-CJH9FXV367, draugelis) move to the vault's Machines list, and leftover users are listed for you to keep or remove. Sessions started under old IDs are re-attributed to you, which also fixes "no longer paired".
